What was the objective(s) of the task?

The objective of this task was to apply core pandas concepts, covered
in the Week 2 lecture on Essentials for Data Analysis, to a real-world

What kind of data did you work with?

The task involved working with a single CSV file, avocado.csv, sourced
from the Kaggle Avocado Prices dataset, containing 18,249 records of
historical avocado prices and sales volumes across 54 U.S. regions

AveragePrice, Total Volume, PLU-coded sales volumes (4046, 4225,

Briefly describe the data science task you

notebook. A new column, Estimated Revenue, was derived by
multiplying Total Volume and AveragePrice. The data was then
grouped by region and type to compute average prices, reshaped using
pivot() to compare prices across regions in wide format, and grouped
by type and year to calculate the standard deviation of Total Bags sold,
in order to assess the consistency of sales volumes over time. Bar plots
were used to visualise summary statistics of Total Volume by year.

How did you analyse the data?

The data was analysed primarily through grouping and aggregation. I
used groupby() on region and type to calculate average prices, then
applied pivot() to reshape this grouped data into a wide format for
direct comparison across regions. Separately, I grouped the data by
type and year to calculate the standard deviation of Total Bags sold,
in order to assess how sales volume consistency changed over the
four years.

Did you use any visualisation techniques to
better understand the data?

Yes, I created a bar plot using plot.bar() to visualise the mean,
median, and standard deviation of Total Volume grouped by year,
which helped illustrate how sales volume and its variability changed
from 2015 to 2018.

What insights did you gain from this
analysis?

This analysis revealed several notable patterns. Organic avocados
were consistently priced higher than conventional ones, averaging
1.65 compared to 1.16, reflecting a typical premium seen in organic
produce markets. Regional variation was also evident, with Houston
recording the lowest average organic price (1.27) among all 54
regions, lower than I initially expected given that organic produce is
generally more uniformly priced across markets. Finally, the
standard deviation of Total Bags sold increased every year from
2015 to 2018 for both avocado types, rising from roughly 7.5×105 to
2.0×106 for conventional and from 2.6×104 to 1.4×105 for organic.
This indicates that avocado sales volumes became progressively less
consistent over time rather than stabilising as the market matured.

One strategy that could reduce or address the
ethical risk.

1. A significant ethical concern with recommendation
systems is algorithmic bias leading to filter bubbles,
where collaborative filtering algorithms
disproportionately promote already-popular items
while under-representing less popular or minorityrelevant content, a phenomenon known as
popularity bias.
2. This narrows the diversity of information and
products users are exposed to over time, reinforcing
existing preferences rather than introducing new
perspectives, and can further marginalise already
underrepresented groups, since data points at the
margins of the distribution often correspond to
minority users. At a societal level, this contributes to
echo chambers, where users are consistently
exposed to content aligning with their existing
viewpoints, reducing exposure to diverse opinions
and potentially reinforcing polarisation.
3. One effective mitigation strategy is diversity-aware
recommendation design, where algorithms are
deliberately adjusted to incorporate diversity
metrics alongside accuracy, ensuring that less
popular or minority-relevant items are still surfaced
to users. Research has shown that diversified
recommendation strategies can measurably reduce
filter bubble effects compared to non-diversified
approaches. This can be complemented by
transparency mechanisms, such as those
