There is one more type of regression: LOWESS regression, or locally weighted scatterplot smoothing. It is a nonparametric regression method used to fit a smooth curve to a scatter plot of data points. Unlike parametric regression methods such as linear regression, which assume a specific functional form for the relationship between dependent and independent variables, LOWESS makes no assumption about the function form. It does not assume that the relationship is linear, quadratic, cubic, or another particular type. Instead, it uses a weighted average of nearby data points to estimate the curve. It considers a window, moves the window across the data along the x-axis, and finds curves that fit the data points in that window. The weights are based on the distance between the point of interest and other points in its neighborhood.
